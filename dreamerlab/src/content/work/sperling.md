## the challenge

A bride-to-be realized her day deserved more than an off-the-shelf template. Discovered through word-of-mouth, she wanted a guest experience that felt unmistakably theirs — cohesive from the first save-the-date to the last thank-you.

## what we did

We built a fully custom brand for the celebration: original illustrations, a complete invitation suite, day-of signage, and a hand-coded wedding site that tells their story better than any template could. Every touchpoint composed to feel like one world.

## the outcome

A guest experience guests remembered — and a couple whose day felt authored, not assembled. *(Case study in progress; full gallery coming soon.)*
