## the challenge

A global superfood brand was losing time and consistency every time a team built a deck from scratch. Every department told the story a little differently, and the creative team had become a bottleneck for work that should have been self-serve.

## what we did

We built a company-wide presentation template system on top of an updated design system — modular, on-brand, and simple enough that non-designers could show up looking polished. Clear rules, reusable components, and guardrails that made the right choice the easy choice.

## the outcome

A scalable system teams adopted immediately and still use today. Less friction, a freed-up creative team, and a confident, consistent way for everyone to speak to what Sambazon offers — value that outlasted the project itself.
